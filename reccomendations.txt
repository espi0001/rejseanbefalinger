# Carins
If the girls wanna start researching I’d reccomend this:
- Day trip to Fitzroy island - search up ferries from cairns to fitroy there’s like 2 main companies I think that do it - rent snorkel gear on the island
- Great Barrier Reef: Scuba you have to book a day trip with one of the dive companies and then can also snorkel on the reef for Noah and Ina if you don’t want to dive - ProDive Cairns are the best for diving but maybe more expensive, I’d just find the cheapest option from the different dive companies in Cairns there like 3-4 options I think 
- Road trip round the waterfall circuit is heaps fun some really amazing waterfalls but need a car for that day (I can tell u which waterfalls to suss)
- Daintree Rainforest day trip (can also do yourself with a car otherwise need to suss tours), if you guys get a car I’ll tell you where to go
- Port Douglas day trip: not as crucial, it’s pretty up there but like doing the islands, the barrier reef, the rainforest and the waterfalls would be my first priorities personally
- Also there’s an hour boat crocodile tour in the Daintree heaps cool, and you can go horse riding on the beach, like I said I’ll send through recco’s if you’re gonna do that day yourself with no tour so just let me know once you’ve looked into it
1 day: Diving Great Barrier Reef
1 day: Daintree rainforest tour
